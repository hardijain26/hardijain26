# 🧠 How I Approach Product

### 01 · 🔎 Discover

Understand the customer problem, context, constraints, and opportunity before defining the solution.

### 02 · 🎯 Prioritize

Evaluate opportunities through:

- Customer impact
- Revenue / business impact
- Merchant growth
- Leadership priorities
- Engineering capacity
- Strategic alignment

### 03 · 🧩 Define

Translate the problem into:

- Product requirements
- User journeys
- Success criteria
- Hypotheses
- Experiments
- Clear product decisions

### 04 · 🤝 Align

Create alignment across:

**Product × Engineering × Design × Business × Leadership**

### 05 · 🚀 Execute

Move from product direction to delivery while managing trade-offs, dependencies, and changing priorities.

### 06 · 📊 Learn

Measure what happened, understand why, and use those insights to improve the product.
